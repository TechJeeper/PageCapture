Manual Mode Instructions (Recommended)

This method works for ANY website (Google, Facebook, etc.).

  1. 1 Open your target website in a new browser tab. You can click here: Open Website
  2. 2 Come back to this page and click Start Recording.
  3. 3 When your browser asks, choose to share the new tab you just opened.
  4. 4 Switch to that tab and scroll manually. The recording will stop automatically after the duration you set.
Note: Most major websites will appear blank here due to security rules. If the site is blank, please switch to Manual Mode.
